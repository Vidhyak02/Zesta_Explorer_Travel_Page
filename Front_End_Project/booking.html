Travel Tips Before You Book

A little planning goes a long way. Keep these essential travel tips in mind before setting off on your next unforgettable adventure.

01 / Essentials

Passport & Visa

Check passport validity, visa requirements and entry regulations for your chosen destination before booking.

02 / Timing

Best Time to Travel

Research seasonal weather, local festivals and peak tourist periods to choose the right time for your trip.

03 / Finances

Plan Your Budget

Consider accommodation, meals, local transport and activities. Keep extra funds for unexpected expenses.

04 / Protection

Travel Insurance

Consider suitable travel insurance for medical emergencies, trip interruptions, baggage loss and unexpected delays.
